# Code of conduct

We want DSHX collaboration to be direct, technical and safe.

Be respectful, provide reproducible evidence, separate observed behavior from assumptions, and accept good-faith correction. Harassment, hateful attacks, credible threats, doxxing, credential theft, malware, impersonation and repeated unsolicited promotion are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and community. Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@dshx.io`. Permanent sanctions and reversals in DSHX Hub go through the approval center and its immutable audit trail.
